title: Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026.

## Scope

This policy covers the Realmroot website at [realmroot.dev](https://realmroot.dev/)
and the official hosted identity, authorization, and capability-discovery service
at [id.realmroot.dev](https://id.realmroot.dev/). It does not cover third-party
self-hosted deployments, whose operators determine their own data practices.
Other products and external services may have separate privacy policies.

The [Terms of Service](/terms/) describe use of the website and official hosted
service. The open-source software is separately licensed under Apache-2.0.

## Information we process

We process account and profile information you provide, authentication and
security data needed to protect your account, Agent identities and controller
relationships, authorization requests and decisions, and technical service
records such as request identifiers, timestamps, operation names, status codes,
and security events.

When you connect an external provider, Realmroot processes the provider account
identifier, granted scopes, and OAuth credentials required to maintain that
connection. Stored provider credentials are encrypted at rest. Authorized Agents can receive
access credentials limited by the approved authorization; this is distinct from
credentials used to maintain a provider connection. Connected external services
may process credentials and requests under their own policies.

## Website visits and cookies

Website requests pass through Cloudflare infrastructure, which processes network
and request information to deliver and protect the site. The hosted identity
service uses cookies to maintain sign-in sessions and support authentication.
Blocking these cookies can prevent sign-in or session features from working.

## How we use information

We use this information to operate and secure the service, authenticate users
and Agents, enforce grants and revocations, exchange short-lived credentials,
investigate failures and abuse, and improve reliability. We do not sell
personal information or use provider credentials for advertising.

## Sharing and processors

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate Realmroot,
with external providers you explicitly connect, when required by law, or when
necessary to protect users and the service. Cloudflare provides hosting,
networking, storage, and security infrastructure for the hosted service.

When you sign in to a connected application, it receives identity information
such as an account identifier and, where authorized, profile information according
to the requested and approved access. Authorized Agents and resource services
receive the identity, authorization information, and request data needed for the
operations you authorize. The receiving application's or provider's privacy
policy governs its subsequent handling of that information.

## Retention and control

We retain information only while it is needed to provide the service, satisfy
security and audit requirements, or comply with law. You can revoke Agent grants and sessions and disconnect external provider
connections from the account controls. Revocation prevents further access through
the revoked authorization; it does not undo completed operations or delete data
already held by connected applications or providers.

Account deletion removes or clears account profile and authentication records and
revokes associated access. Some identifiers and reduced authorization or audit
records remain to preserve security and record integrity. External revocation and
stored-file cleanup can finish after local account deletion. Deleting a Realmroot
account does not delete your accounts or data at external providers.

## Security and changes

We use encryption, short-lived credentials, proof-of-possession tokens, access
controls, and audit records to protect service data. No system can guarantee
absolute security. Material changes to this policy will be published on this
page with a new effective date.

For general questions, use the [Realmroot repository](https://github.com/realmroot/realmroot/issues).
GitHub issues are public: do not post personal information, credentials, or
account-deletion evidence there.
